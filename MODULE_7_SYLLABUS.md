# Module 7 — 2-Day / 8-Session Training Syllabus (Job Architecture & Competency Modeling)

## 1. Context

Module 7 covers Service 7: Job Architecture & Competency Modeling — mapped from Q11 and Q12 in the diagnostic ("Apakah Deskripsi Jabatan didukung oleh Model Kompetensi yang terdefinisi (Inti, Fungsional, Kepemimpinan) yang menetapkan tingkat kemahiran yang diperlukan?", "Apakah proses rekrutmen menggunakan alat penyaringan berbasis kompetensi yang terstruktur (misalnya, Behavioral Event Interviewing), bukan seleksi ad-hoc?"). It builds a layer underneath both the business-systems modules (2–5) and Module 6's people layer: Module 6 assumes a business already knows what "good" looks like for a role and just needs to set goals against it, coach toward it, and correct away from it — but most SME businesses never actually defined "good" in the first place. Their job descriptions are task lists ("jawab telepon, susun laporan, follow up klien"), not competency profiles with proficiency levels, and their hiring runs on "orangnya kelihatan cocok" instead of any structured method for testing whether a candidate actually has what the role needs. Module 7 fixes that at the root: it builds a real competency model (Kompetensi Inti/Fungsional/Kepemimpinan with defined proficiency levels) that job descriptions can actually be built on, then rebuilds recruitment around structured, competency-based screening — Behavioral Event Interviewing and a scoring rubric — so hiring stops being a coin flip dressed up as an interview. A reader who's completed Module 6 gets sharper, better-anchored language for goals and PIPs by doing this module too, since a goal or an improvement plan is only as good as the competency standard it's measured against — but this module is fully self-contained for a reader who hasn't touched Module 6 at all.

Same as Modules 2–6, this is an 8-chapter build, one per session of a 2-day / 8-session in-class training, matching the established format exactly (`Module 6/src/content/chapter1.ts` through `chapter8.ts`, `Module 6/src/types/card.ts`, its progress store).

Voice and mechanics carry over unchanged: casual, blunt, direct Bahasa Indonesia; reuse existing card types (`info`, `yes-no`, `multiple-choice`, `fill-in-the-blank`, `sort-list`, `reflection`, `finding-review`, `worksheet`) freely; capture exercises in-app via `reflection` cards tied to the module's own workbook store, not static PDF downloads; target 10–20 cards per chapter; close with Chapter 8 compiling everything into one downloadable report via the `report` card type.

## 2. The 8-chapter syllabus

**Hari 1 — Membangun Arsitektur Kompetensi**

1. **Kenapa Deskripsi Jabatan yang Cuma Daftar Tugas Selalu Berujung Salah Rekrut — Jebakan "Kelihatannya Cocok"** — Kenapa job desc yang isinya cuma daftar tugas dan rekrutmen yang mengandalkan insting/chemistry di ruang interview selalu berujung salah rekrut cepat atau lambat; memperkenalkan dua tahap arsitektur jabatan — model kompetensi, lalu rekrutmen terstruktur di atasnya — sebagai peta bab-bab berikutnya.
2. **Peta Arsitektur Jabatan: Dari Model Kompetensi ke Rekrutmen Berbasis Bukti** — Peta besar dua tahap arsitektur jabatan dan kenapa kebanyakan bisnis SME melompat langsung ke tahap rekrutmen tanpa pernah menyelesaikan tahap pertama, plus self-assessment posisi bisnis reader di tiap tahap — dibongkar satu-satu di Bab 3-6.
3. **Membangun Model Kompetensi: Kompetensi Inti, Fungsional, dan Kepemimpinan** — Tiga lapis model kompetensi dan teknik menurunkannya dari apa yang benar-benar dibutuhkan sebuah peran untuk berhasil, bukan dari template generik yang ditempel begitu saja.
4. **Menetapkan Tingkat Kemahiran — Dari Deskripsi Jabatan ke Standar yang Bisa Diukur** — Teknik mengubah tiap kompetensi jadi skala tingkat kemahiran dengan anchor perilaku yang bisa diamati, biar deskripsi jabatan berhenti jadi daftar tugas kabur dan jadi standar yang bisa diukur.

**Hari 2 — Memperbaiki Rekrutmen dengan Arsitektur yang Sudah Dibangun**

5. **Mendiagnosis Rekrutmen Ad-Hoc — Kenapa "Kelihatannya Cocok" Bukan Metode Seleksi** — Teknik mendiagnosis akar masalah proses rekrutmen yang ad-hoc lewat tiga lensa: kriteria kompetensi yang nggak pernah didefinisikan, interview yang nggak benar-benar mengetes kriteria itu, atau penilaian kandidat yang nggak konsisten dibanding-bandingkan — dijangkarkan ke satu posisi nyata yang sedang atau akan direkrut reader.
6. **Merancang Alat Penyaringan Berbasis Kompetensi: Behavioral Event Interviewing dan Rubrik Seleksi** — Teknik Behavioral Event Interviewing (menggali contoh perilaku nyata di masa lalu, bukan jawaban hipotetis) dan rubrik penilaian terstruktur yang diturunkan langsung dari model kompetensi dan tingkat kemahiran Bab 3-4.
7. **Lokakarya Arsitektur Jabatan: Merancang Profil Kompetensi dan Alat Seleksi untuk Satu Peran** — Sesi praktik menyatukan model kompetensi, tingkat kemahiran, diagnosis, dan alat BEI/rubrik jadi satu paket arsitektur jabatan yang lengkap dan siap pakai untuk satu peran nyata di bisnis reader.
8. **Menginstal Arsitektur Jabatan — Kepemilikan, Ritme Update, dan Laporan Lengkap** — Menetapkan pemilik tiap bagian arsitektur, izin untuk mulai dari satu peran dulu, menghubungkan ritme update-nya ke sistem yang sudah ada (Modul 6), dan mengunduh laporan lengkap dari Bab 1-7.

## 3. Notes for chapter build prompts

- Each chapter above becomes its own `MODULE_7_CHAPTER_N_REBUILD_PROMPT.md`, following the exact section structure of the prior modules' `CHAPTER_N_REBUILD_PROMPT.md` files (Context → syllabus list with target chapter bolded → chapter objective → workbook capture → voice & format → out of scope → definition of done).
- Module 7 is its own sibling app, `Module 7/`, scaffolded from `Module 6/`'s conventions — but unlike Modules 2–6, the scaffolding itself is done by `scaffold-module.ps1` (a plain copy + find/replace script), not by Claude Code manually reading and rewriting the shared primitive files. Run `scaffold-module.ps1 -SourceModule 6 -TargetModule 7 -SourceTitle "Performance Management System Overhaul" -TargetTitle "Job Architecture & Competency Modeling"` before Chapter 1's build (the chained runner script does this automatically). Chapter 1's rebuild prompt should **not** include file-porting instructions — it should state the scaffold already exists and cover only chapter1.ts content authorship plus registering it in `chapters.ts`.
- No new card types should be required — `reflection`, `finding-review`-style scoring, and `report` already exist and should be reused rather than rebuilt.
- Keep Module 7 scoped to *job architecture and competency-based recruitment* (defining competency models, proficiency levels, structured screening) — this is a different axis than Module 6's people-management cycle (goal-setting/coaching/corrective action is Module 6's job, not Module 7's); reference Module 6 as optional connective tissue (e.g. competency models and proficiency levels feeding sharper goal language and PIP standards) but never assume the reader has done it — keep each chapter self-contained for a reader who hasn't touched Module 6 at all.
- Chapters 5-7 should stay anchored to one specific, real role or hiring case the reader names in Chapter 5 — don't let the screening-tool design in Chapter 6 or the integration workshop in Chapter 7 drift into abstract HR-policy-writing disconnected from that one real case, the same way Modules 4-6 keep their fix chapters anchored to one real process/SOP/team-member case.
- Chapter 8's closing card should carry no `cta` field — since Module 3's Chapter 1, the deck footer's "Selesai Bab Ini" button already handles returning to the module home screen; the same convention Modules 3-6's Chapter 8 follow.
