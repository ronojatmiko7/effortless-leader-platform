# Module 9 — 2-Day / 8-Session Training Syllabus (AI Adoption & Claude for Business)

## 1. Context

Module 9 is different from Modules 2-8 in one important way: it doesn't map to a single Q-number in the 13-question diagnostic. Modules 2-8 each fix one specific Service flagged by one specific diagnostic question. Module 9 is a bonus/cross-cutting module — it doesn't diagnose a gap, it teaches a capability that makes every other module (and every other part of the business) faster to execute: adopting AI, specifically Claude for Business (Claude Team/Enterprise plans, Claude Projects, Artifacts, and Claude Cowork for file/task automation), in a disciplined way instead of the ad-hoc "suruh tim coba AI" pattern most SME owners are currently stuck in.

The problem this module solves is the same shape as Module 8's, one layer earlier: businesses don't fail to adopt AI because the tool is bad. They fail because AI adoption repeats the exact graveyard pattern Module 8 already named — dibeli/dicoba sekali, dipakai satu-dua minggu oleh satu-dua orang penasaran, lalu diam-diam ditinggalkan begitu kesibukan sehari-hari kembali normal, tanpa siapa pun pernah benar-benar memutuskan alur kerja mana yang cocok dipasangi AI, siapa yang bertanggung jawab mengecek hasilnya, dan bagaimana caranya itu jadi kebiasaan tim, bukan mainan satu orang. Module 9 gives the reader a way to pick the right first use case, roll it out with proper human checks, and install it as a habit — using Claude for Business as the concrete tool, not AI-in-the-abstract.

A reader who's completed Module 8 gets Module 9 as a direct, explicit application of that same change-management discipline to one specific kind of change: adopting an AI tool. Module 9 references that connection where natural (Chapters 1, 6, and 8 especially) but is fully self-contained — a reader who's touched no other module in this funnel can walk in cold, pick one real workflow in their business, and get a complete first AI adoption package by Chapter 8.

Because product screens and exact feature names change faster than this module's shelf life should, keep every reference to Claude for Business at the judgment/capability level (what a Team/Enterprise plan gives an organization — centralized admin, access control, shared Projects, Cowork automation across files — and what a reader should look for and ask when evaluating it) rather than describing specific buttons, menus, or UI steps that will go stale. Never invent pricing, feature names, or capabilities not already grounded in this brief or the actual Module 9 chapter prompts.

Same as Modules 2-8, this is an 8-chapter build, one per session of a 2-day / 8-session in-class training, matching the established format exactly (`Module 8/src/content/chapter1.ts` through `chapter8.ts`, `Module 8/src/types/card.ts`, its progress store).

Voice and mechanics carry over unchanged: casual, blunt, direct Bahasa Indonesia; reuse existing card types (`info`, `yes-no`, `multiple-choice`, `fill-in-the-blank`, `sort-list`, `reflection`, `finding-review`, `worksheet`, `report`) freely; capture exercises in-app via `reflection` cards tied to the module's own workbook store, not static PDF downloads; target 10-20 cards per chapter; close with Chapter 8 compiling everything into one downloadable report via the `report` card type.

## 2. The 8-chapter syllabus

**Hari 1 — Memahami dan Memilih Kasus Pakai AI**

1. **"Suruh Tim Coba AI" Bukan Strategi — Kenapa Eksperimen AI Acak Berakhir di Kuburan yang Sama dengan Tool Lain** — Kenapa menyuruh tim "coba-coba pakai AI" tanpa arah berakhir persis seperti tool dan SOP lain yang pernah dibeli lalu ditinggalkan: satu-dua orang penasaran pakai buat hal receh, sisanya nggak pernah nyentuh, dan nggak ada yang bisa jawab "AI ini sebenarnya bantu apa buat bisnis kita." Memperkenalkan dua tahap arsitektur adopsi AI — memilih & menyiapkan kasus pakai, lalu menjalankan & menginstal kebiasaan — sebagai peta bab-bab berikutnya, dan memperkenalkan Claude for Business sebagai alat konkret yang dipakai sepanjang modul ini, bukan "AI" secara abstrak.
2. **Peta AI untuk Bisnis Anda: dari Chat Individu ke Alur Kerja Organisasi** — Peta besar tiga level pemakaian AI di bisnis — level individu (satu orang pakai chat AI buat draft receh), level tim (kasus pakai berulang dengan konteks bersama), level organisasi (akun bisnis dengan kontrol akses, admin, dan automasi lintas file/tugas) — plus self-assessment posisi bisnis reader di tiap level sekarang, dibongkar satu-satu di Bab 3-8.
3. **Memilih Kasus Pakai yang Tepat — Kenapa "AI untuk Semua Hal" Gagal Duluan** — Kerangka memilih kasus pakai pertama: seberapa sering pekerjaan itu berulang, seberapa mahal/lama kalau dikerjakan manual, dan seberapa mudah hasilnya dicek benar-salah oleh manusia sebelum dipakai — plus jebakan mencoba pasang AI ke semua proses sekaligus, atau ke keputusan besar yang susah diverifikasi.
4. **Data, Keamanan, dan Kepercayaan Tim** — Batasan dasar data apa yang boleh dan nggak boleh dimasukkan ke alat AI, kenapa kontrol akses di level organisasi (bukan akun pribadi tiap orang) penting, dan sumber resistensi yang paling sering diabaikan CEO: ketakutan tim bahwa AI akan menggantikan pekerjaan mereka — kalau nggak dijawab langsung di awal, tim diam-diam menghindari atau mensabotase alat itu.

**Hari 2 — Menjalankan dan Menginstal Kebiasaan AI**

5. **Merancang Alur Kerja AI Pertama Anda dengan Claude for Business** — Reader mengunci satu kasus pakai nyata dari Bab 3, lalu merancang alur kerja konkret: siapa yang pakai, jenis pemakaian Claude for Business yang paling cocok (chat biasa, ruang kerja bersama/Projects untuk konteks berulang, atau automasi lintas file/tugas lewat Cowork), langkah cek manusia sebelum hasilnya dipakai beneran, dan target keberhasilan sederhana yang bisa diukur.
6. **Menjalankan & Melatih Tim Memakai AI di Lapangan** — Teknik menjaga alur kerja AI yang baru berjalan dengan benar — cara memberi instruksi/prompt yang jelas vs yang ambigu, cara memverifikasi hasil AI sebelum dipakai (bukan ditelan mentah-mentah), menangani momen "AI-nya salah" secara konstruktif bukan menghukum, dan mengenali sinyal tim mulai diam-diam balik ke cara manual sepenuhnya.
7. **Lokakarya AI: Merancang Paket Adopsi AI Lengkap untuk Satu Alur Kerja Nyata** — Sesi praktik menyatukan kasus pakai, alur kerja, langkah pelatihan, dan langkah verifikasi jadi satu paket adopsi AI yang lengkap dan siap pakai untuk satu alur kerja nyata di bisnis reader.
8. **Menginstal Kebiasaan AI di Organisasi — Ritme Evaluasi, Kepemilikan, dan Laporan Lengkap** — Menetapkan pemilik yang secara berkala mengecek kualitas dan kepatuhan pemakaian AI, ritme evaluasi setelah rollout awal, izin untuk memulai dari satu alur kerja dulu sebelum memperluas ke alur kerja lain, koneksi eksplisit ke disiplin change management Modul 8, dan mengunduh laporan lengkap dari Bab 1-7.

## 3. Notes for chapter build prompts

- Each chapter above becomes its own `MODULE_9_CHAPTER_N_REBUILD_PROMPT.md`, following the exact section structure of the prior modules' `CHAPTER_N_REBUILD_PROMPT.md` files (Context → syllabus list with target chapter bolded → chapter objective → workbook capture → voice & format → out of scope → definition of done).
- Module 9 is its own sibling app, `Module 9/`, scaffolded from `Module 8/`'s conventions via `scaffold-module.ps1` (a plain copy + find/replace script), not by Claude Code manually reading and rewriting the shared primitive files. Run `scaffold-module.ps1 -SourceModule 8 -TargetModule 9 -SourceTitle "Enterprise Change Management Rollout" -TargetTitle "AI Adoption & Claude for Business"` before Chapter 1's build (the chained runner script does this automatically). Chapter 1's rebuild prompt should **not** include file-porting instructions — it should state the scaffold already exists and cover only chapter1.ts content authorship plus registering it in `chapters.ts`.
- No new card types should be required — `reflection`, `finding-review`-style scoring, `yes-no`, and `report` already exist and should be reused rather than rebuilt.
- Keep every mention of Claude for Business at the capability/judgment level — what a business-grade AI account gives an organization (centralized admin and access control, shared team workspaces, automation across files/tasks) and what to look for and ask about, not specific buttons, menus, screenshots, or exact feature names that will go stale. Never invent pricing or capabilities not already in this syllabus.
- Keep Module 9 scoped to *choosing and rolling out AI use cases responsibly* (prioritization, data/trust basics, workflow design, training, verification, reinforcement) — not to teaching prompt-engineering mechanics in depth or turning into a generic "what is AI" explainer. Reference Module 8 as optional connective tissue (a reader who's done it already has the change-management muscle this module applies) but never assume it was done — keep each chapter self-contained for a reader who hasn't touched any earlier module at all.
- Chapters 5-7 should stay anchored to one specific, real AI use case (a real recurring task in the reader's business — customer service replies, weekly report summaries, SOP drafts, competitor research, marketing draft copy, or anything else genuinely recurring) the reader names/locks in Chapter 5 — don't let the workflow design in Chapter 5, the field execution in Chapter 6, or the workshop in Chapter 7 drift into abstract AI-strategy talk disconnected from that one real case, the same way Modules 4-8 keep their fix chapters anchored to one real process/SOP/team-member/role/change case.
- Chapter 8's closing card should carry no `cta` field — since Module 3's Chapter 1, the deck footer's "Selesai Bab Ini" button already handles returning to the module home screen; the same convention Modules 3-8's Chapter 8 follow.
